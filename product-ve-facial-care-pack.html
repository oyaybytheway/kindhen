Volcanic Earth makes its skincare in Port Vila, on the island of Efate in Vanuatu. Every bottle in this pack is made with tamanu oil, pressed from the cured nuts of the tamanu tree (Calophyllum inophyllum) — an oil the Ni-Vanuatu people have used in traditional skin care for generations.

It's a simple three-step routine: cleanse, tone, moisturize. Virgin coconut oil, olive oil and vegetable glycerine help skin feel soft and moisturized, and witch hazel in the toner leaves it feeling fresh. A steady daily routine like this can help skin look smoother and clearer-looking, feel firmer-looking, and soften the look of fine lines.

Three full-size bottles from a real island brand make a thoughtful gift for a mom, a sister, a friend — or for you, at the end of a long day.

Ingredients

Facial Cleanser (135 ml): Aqua (pure Vanuatu water), Virgin Coconut Oil (Cocus Nucifera), White Clay (Kaolinite), Emulsifying Wax Profile (nf), Vitamin E (dl-Alpha Tocopheryl Acetate), Potassium Sorbate, Tamanu Oil (Calophyllum Inophyllum Oil), Lavender Essential Oil (Lavandula Dentata), Rose Geranium Essential Oil (Pelargonium Graveolens).

Facial Toner (135 ml): Aqua (pure Vanuatu water), Witch Hazel (Hamamelis Virginiana), Geogard ECT, Tamanu Oil (Calophyllum Inophyllum Oil), Lavender Essential Oil (Lavandula Dentata), Rose Geranium Essential Oil (Pelargonium Graveolens).

Facial Moisturizer (135 ml): Aqua (pure Vanuatu water), Virgin Coconut Oil (Cocus Nucifera), Olive Oil (Olea Europaea), Tamanu Oil (Calophyllum Inophyllum Oil), Emulsifying Wax Profile (nf), Vegetable Glycerine Liquid (Vegetable bp), Geogard ECT, Vitamin E (dl-Alpha Tocopheryl Acetate), Lavender Essential Oil (Lavandula Dentata), Rose Geranium Essential Oil (Pelargonium Graveolens).

Ingredient lists as published by Volcanic Earth on their product page (checked Nov 12, 2026). Contains lavender and rose geranium essential oils.
