Shipping & returns

Draft policy. Hen isn't open for orders yet. This page shows the policy we plan to launch with; final details will be confirmed with our suppliers and carriers before the first order.

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping to addresses in the contiguous United States — that's the shipping we cover, and it's already included in every listed price.
  • Most orders ship from US-based warehouse partners. One exception is noted below.
  • You'll see an estimated delivery window before you pay, and a tracking link once your order ships.
  • If you'd like express on an eligible item, request it in your basket. It's an upcharge you pay; we only cover standard delivery. Enter your delivery ZIP and we'll add the extra for your location automatically. How the upcharge is calculated.
  • We're sorry — we can't offer express shipping on the Hen Family Throw, and we apologize for the inconvenience. That throw ships by the standard delivery already included in the price.
  • Holidays: we can't promise arrival by a specific holiday until carrier cutoffs and stock are confirmed — even with express. If timing matters, write to us before you order and we'll tell you honestly what we know.

Shipped from the South Pacific

The Essential Facial Care Pack with Tamanu Oil is made by Volcanic Earth in Vanuatu and ships from Vanuatu or Australia (Vanuatu Post or Australia Post), not from a US warehouse.

  • Standard shipping is included in the $99.99 price — no separate shipping charge from us.
  • Allow 24–48 hours for processing, then typically 10–14 days to the US, sometimes up to 21 working days.
  • Import duties or charges may apply when the parcel enters the US.
  • Because of the distance, we can't promise arrival by any holiday.

Printed to order in the United States

The Hen Family Throw is printed to order in the United States. Standard shipping is included in the $49.99 price.

  • Allow about 2–5 business days to make it, then about 2–5 business days by USPS — typically 3–7 business days in all.
  • Orders to Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico usually add 7–12 business days.
  • This exclusive Mama Hen throw is only offered through December 9, 2026 — a holiday run, and not for long.
  • Because each throw is made to order, we can't promise arrival by a holiday.
  • We're sorry — we can't offer express shipping on the Hen Family Throw, and we apologize for the inconvenience.

Express shipping, if you request it

Every listed price already includes standard delivery — that's the shipping we cover. If you'd like express on an eligible item, request it in your basket. Enter your delivery ZIP and we'll automatically add the express upcharge for that location, shown on the order summary before you pay.

  • We're sorry — we can't offer express shipping on the Hen Family Throw, and we apologize for the inconvenience. That throw ships by the standard delivery already included in the $49.99 price.
  • The Essential Facial Care Pack ships from the South Pacific and does not have an express option we can quote. If it's in your basket with other items, express is calculated only for the items we can send that way; the pack stays on included standard shipping.
  • For eligible items, the upcharge is the difference between USPS Priority Mail commercial rates (effective October 4, 2026) and the standard shipping already included in the price. You pay only that extra — or nothing extra, if Priority for your ZIP is at or below the standard we've already included.
  • The amount depends on where the parcel is going. Until we confirm the exact warehouse city for those items, we quote the farthest lower-48 zone so the upcharge isn't short. If the real extra is less when you pay, you'll be charged the lower amount.
  • We only place an express shipment when our fulfiller can actually send that item by Priority Mail. If they can't, you won't be charged the upcharge.
  • Even with express, we can't promise arrival by a holiday. Transit to Alaska, Hawaii, or Puerto Rico can still take longer.

Returns

  • 30 days from delivery to start a return for unused items in their original packaging.
  • Damaged, faulty or wrong item? Send us a photo within 7 days and we'll replace it or refund you, at no cost to you.
  • For change-of-mind returns, we'll send instructions; return postage may be deducted from the refund (to be finalized before launch).
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method once the return is received.

Prices

Our prices are everyday prices. We don't show made-up "was" prices or countdown clocks, and holiday banners on this site only ever show the same prices you'll find on the product pages.
