Our story

A hen looks after her nest

Every family has one: the person who reads the reviews twice, keeps a list on the fridge, and knows a fair price when she sees one. She isn't cheap — she just won't let the nest get taken for a ride.

Hen is named for her. Our mascot is a mother hen who looks after everyone: the chicks with their tablets and their brand-new jeans, Papa with his football, and the dog and cat curled up by the window.

We started Hen for moms on a budget who want good things without the guesswork. That means a small shelf, products we sample first, plain-language policies, and prices that feel right the moment you see them.

Hen is American-minded and based on American soil. We like to support American businesses, and veterans and the families who stand with them are always welcome here.
