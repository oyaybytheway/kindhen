Looking after your information

Privacy

This page explains what Hen collects, why, and how you can make choices. We use plain language, and we'll update this page if our practices change.

What we collect on this website

  • Your basket is saved on your device only (in your browser), so it is still there if you come back later. It is not sent to us until you place an order.
  • Messages you send us — for example through our contact page, a product-request note, or an email to support@kindhen.com — so we can reply.
  • Email sign-ups if you choose to join our optional list, as described below.
  • Order details when you buy something: your name, email, shipping address, and what you ordered, so we can fulfill the order and send you updates, and — if you have signed up or opted in — so we can personalize offers, as described below.

Email sign-ups and personalized offers

The email sign-up on this site is optional. If you sign up, we add your address to our subscriber list so we can email you monthly coupons, news about new products and store updates, and personalized offers and product recommendations.

We use your email, and what you buy from Hen, to choose those offers and recommendations. They may be from Hen or from our related stores. We don't sell your personal information. You can unsubscribe at any time; every marketing email will include a way to do that, or you can write to support@kindhen.com and ask to be removed.

When checkout opens, we also plan to let you opt in to these emails at pay time, separately from the site sign-up.

Until our newsletter list is fully connected, a sign-up may open a message to support@kindhen.com so we can add you by hand. Once the list is live, sign-ups go to that list and can be exported if we need a copy.

Payments

When you place an order, payment is handled by Stripe. Card numbers and other payment details go to Stripe, not to a card form on kindhen.com. Stripe's own privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy. After you pay, Stripe sends you a receipt by email.

Cookies and advertising tags

The site is built so we can add advertising tags from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Pinterest, and Google. Those tags are only loaded when we have turned them on by placing an official tag ID in our site code. When they are on, they typically set cookies or similar storage on your device and may collect information such as:

  • The pages you visit on kindhen.com, including product pages
  • Actions such as adding an item to the basket, starting checkout, or completing a purchase
  • A technical identifier for your browser or device, and information your browser sends (for example, IP address, browser type, and approximate location)
  • How you arrived at Hen, including from an ad you were shown

We use that information to understand how people find Hen, to measure whether our ads are working, and — where the advertising services allow it — to show Hen ads to people who may be interested. We do not invent reviews or hide these tools.

If the tags are not yet turned on, this site does not load those advertising scripts. The options below still apply once they are.

How to opt out

You can limit advertising cookies and some personalized ads through the official tools of each service, and through industry opt-outs:

  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): Ad settings and Meta's privacy policy
  • Pinterest: Personalized ads on Pinterest and your Pinterest settings
  • Google: Google ad settings, Google's privacy policy, and the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on
  • Industry opt-outs: Digital Advertising Alliance and the Network Advertising Initiative

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some site features (such as a saved basket) may not work as expected.

How long we keep information

Order and support records are kept as long as we need them to fulfill an order, handle a return, keep required tax or accounting records, or answer a question you asked. Subscriber list records are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and for a short time afterward if we need them to honor that request or keep required records. Advertising services keep information under their own policies.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, write to the nest or email support@kindhen.com.
